# Albert Chan · A career in motion

An interactive career portfolio with recessed floral lettering, an ivory and slate palette, an animated avatar that shrinks into a full-body leaning pose, a hanging crew pass, and a scroll-driven rowing course.

The headline reads “I help clients build a competitive advantage with trusted AI.” Career chapters run from Rowland AI through Meta, Tickr, two Google roles, and P&G, with resume highlights visible throughout. Rowland features the Chatbot to Agentic Org Chart takeaway and ChatGPT wording. Provider logos, customer logos, and national flags share the oval skill tag groups. Logo panels use transparent fills, and Meta’s group includes the Accenture wordmark. The Rowland takeaway stays on one line. A visible highlight describes Albert’s leadership in the company’s go-to-market repositioning from a chatbot to an Agentic Org Chart.

## Run locally

Serve the published directory with Python.

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Open `http://localhost:8765` in your browser.

## Interactions

- The name is centered above a horizontal career banner.
- The avatar waves automatically and changes its viewing direction during idle periods.
- Scrolling shrinks the portrait into a full-body pose leaning on the final letter. Scrolling upward restores the greeting.
- Select “Wave hello” to repeat the greeting, or use the direction buttons.
- Move the cursor across the hero for directional response.
- Scroll through the career course to move the boat between small company pennants anchored directly on the river checkpoint dots.
- Select an employer logo to jump to that career chapter.
- Drag or flip the crew pass inside section 02.
- Pause decorative motion with the header control.
- Only the video players produce audio. Page background music has been removed.

The avatar uses layered illustrations, with a continuous hand-sway effect, breathing animation, and directional portraits. The wave uses the intact portrait, keeping the shoulder and arm connected. System reduced-motion preferences are respected, and offscreen avatar animation pauses.

Video previews retain their recorded speech and embedded montage tracks.
